Questions

Contact support.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. Download speech models in the app first. To refine and summarize offline, install Ollama and a local language model, then select them in Settings.

Does it need Accessibility permission?

Accessibility permission lets Macomprendo read selected text and paste results into the focused field. Without it, dictation copies the result for you to paste by hand.

What does it cost?

The app and local processing are free. A paid provider may charge for requests if you configure one.

Is it open source?

Yes. The app source uses the MIT licence. The distributed build is GPL-3.0 because it includes espeak-ng. The licensing page explains the bundled components.
